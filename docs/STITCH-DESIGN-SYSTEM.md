## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the confluence of distinguished academic publishing and precision decision-intelligence. Built for ambitious global scholars, university delegates, and admissions strategists, the experience commands gravitas, clarity, and unhurried intellectual rigor.

The aesthetic direction rejects transient consumer SaaS tropes—no neon gradients, oversized bubble radii, or hyper-saturated accents. Instead, it pairs structural editorial tension with architectural calm. Crisp typographic hierarchies, warm bespoke canvas tones, and restrained linear boundaries evoke scholarly journals, bespoke binders, and archival atlases, while preserving modern web responsiveness and data density. The emotional tone is authoritative, warm, and elevated.

## Colors

The palette balances warm, tactile academic tones with deep, prestigious accents:

- **Primary & Tonal Anchor:** Primary Burgundy (`#70263D`) and Deep Burgundy (`#4B1728`) serve as the core brand foundation. Use for primary action states, authoritative banners, prominent data markers, and header highlights.
- **Canvas & Containers:** Base canvas is Warm Cream (`#F7F2E9`), avoiding the clinical glare of pure white. Surfaces, cards, and data drawers sit slightly elevated on Soft Ivory (`#FFFDF9`).
- **Accent & Metallics:** Soft Champagne (`#D8BD91`) provides subtle, prestigious highlights, verification seals, and milestone indicators.
- **Status & Domain Classification:**
  - Pastel Sage (`#DCE6DA`): Acceptance criteria, funded statuses, verified documentation.
  - Pale Lavender (`#E8E2F0`): Academic disciplines, scholarly research streams, institutional categorization.
  - Dusty Rose (`#E8CDD2`) & Muted Blush (`#F3E3E5`): Visa milestones, subtle tag backgrounds, secondary highlights.
- **Typography & Boundaries:** Primary text reads in Charcoal Ink (`#30272A`), delivering dense contrast without harsh absolute black. Secondary metadata utilizes Muted Umber (`#786D70`). Structural separators rely on Border Stone/Rose (`#E7DDE0`).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the structured geometry of Manrope for headers and metrics with the functional clarity of Inter for dense analytical reading, search filters, and tabular displays.

Where multilingual contexts demand Bangla localization, pair with Noto Sans Bengali, matching optical line-heights and baseline metrics to maintain editorial rhythm.

- **Headlines & Titles:** Set in Manrope with slight negative tracking to produce tight, commanding editorial anchors.
- **Body Text:** Set in Inter with generous line-heights (1.6x) against Warm Cream or Soft Ivory backgrounds for prolonged study sessions and document review.
- **Labels & Micro-data:** Micro-copy, metadata headers, and category tags utilize Inter uppercase with positive letter spacing (`0.04em` - `0.05em`) to evoke formal cataloging.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a controlled, fixed-fluid editorial grid:

- **Desktop (12 Columns):** 1200px to 1440px max content container, centered with a desktop margin of `3.5rem` and `2rem` gutters. Multi-column views balance institutional overviews (8 columns) against admission metrics and checklist modules (4 columns).
- **Tablet (8 Columns):** Fluid margin of `2rem` with `1.5rem` gutters. Sidebars fold into top-level contextual drawers.
- **Mobile (4 Columns):** Fluid margin of `1.25rem` with `1rem` gutters. Information stacks linearly to maintain single-column legibility for academic tables and pathway checklists.
- **Spacing Rhythm:** Base unit is 4px. Structural section breaks leverage `space-xl` (`2.5rem`), while form controls and modular card interiors rely strictly on `space-sm` (`0.5rem`) to `space-md` (`1rem`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on architectural surfaces and low-contrast outlines rather than heavy blurred drop shadows:

- **Tonal Layering:** The foundational layer rests on Warm Cream (`#F7F2E9`). Content modules, dossiers, and academic profiles elevate to Soft Ivory (`#FFFDF9`). Flyout modals and transient dropdowns sit on elevated Ivory with crisp linear boundaries.
- **Outlines over Drops:** Surfaces are delineated by crisp 1px borders in Border Stone/Rose (`#E7DDE0`). High-density data tables and cards avoid blurred drop-shadows entirely, creating an intentional, fine-print journal appearance.
- **Subtle Ambient Lift:** When transient overlays, navigation flyouts, or tooltips must lift above the canvas, use an ultra-subtle, warm-tinted shadow: `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(48, 39, 42, 0.06), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(48, 39, 42, 0.04)`.

## Shapes

The design system enforces a restrained, architectural geometry (`ROUND_FOUR` / `ROUND_EIGHT`):

- **Structural Containers & Cards:** Base corner radius is `0.25rem` (4px) or `0.5rem` (8px). This reinforces the prestige of printed certificates, academic records, and index cards.
- **Inputs & Interactive Controls:** Standard form fields and buttons adopt a strict `0.25rem` (4px) to `0.375rem` (6px) corner radius.
- **Tags and Badges:** Avoid full capsule/pill shapes. Status tags, university rankings, and country markers are set with a crisp `0.25rem` (4px) corner radius, preserving a systematic and disciplined interface.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background in Primary Burgundy (`#70263D`), text in Soft Ivory (`#FFFDF9`), border in `#70263D`. Hover shifts to Deep Burgundy (`#4B1728`). Height: 40px (desktop) / 44px (touch). Padding: `0.75rem 1.25rem`. Border radius: 4px.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Background in transparent, text in Deep Burgundy (`#4B1728`), 1px solid border in Border Stone/Rose (`#E7DDE0`). Hover shifts background to Muted Blush (`#F3E3E5`).
- **Tertiary / Academic Action:** Background in Soft Champagne (`#D8BD91`), text in Deep Burgundy (`#4B1728`). Reserved for scholarship actions and application milestones.

### Chips & Status Badges
- Non-pill layout: strictly 4px radius, uppercase label styling, 11px font size, `0.25rem 0.5rem` padding.
- **Verified/Scholarship:** Pastel Sage (`#DCE6DA`) fill with `#2D482B` text.
- **Program/Faculty:** Pale Lavender (`#E8E2F0`) fill with `#3D3054` text.
- **Deadline/Requirement:** Muted Blush (`#F3E3E5`) fill with `#70263D` text.

### Input Fields & Selects
- Background in Soft Ivory (`#FFFDF9`), 1px border in Border Stone/Rose (`#E7DDE0`), text in Charcoal Ink (`#30272A`).
- Focus state: 1px outline in Primary Burgundy (`#70263D`) with no high-glow ring; optional 1px subtle inset accent. Placeholder text set in Secondary Text (`#786D70`). Height: 42px.

### Lists & Institutional Data Tables
- Clean hairline dividers: 1px solid `#E7DDE0`. Alternating rows remain unshaded or shift delicately between Soft Ivory and Warm Cream.
- Header rows: uppercase `label-sm` typography in Secondary Text (`#786D70`), no colored backgrounds, bottom border weight of 1.5px.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- 16px square (checkbox) with 2px corner radius; 16px circle (radio).
- Border: 1.5px solid `#786D70`. Selected state: fill with Primary Burgundy (`#70263D`) bearing a crisp white mark.

### Cards & Intelligence Dossiers
- Background in Soft Ivory (`#FFFDF9`), framed by 1px Border Stone/Rose (`#E7DDE0`), with zero heavy drop-shadows.
- Header sections feature a thin divider line and a champagne highlight tag (`#D8BD91`) or editorial Manrope subhead.

### Additional Domain Components
- **Pathway Milestones:** Stepper with 1.5px architectural rules connecting 12px diamond nodes (Burgundy for complete, Champagne for in-progress, Border Stone for future).
- **Scholarship Quotient Indicator:** A linear progress bar featuring an Ivory track, 1px border, and a solid Deep Burgundy fill with Soft Champagne metric markers.
