Bavarian Formula Conversion for Bangladesh:

Grade = 1 + 3 × [(4.00 - 3.24) / (4.00 - 2.00)] = 2.14 (German Academic Scale)

*A German grade of 2.14 satisfies the preliminary academic threshold (≤ 2.50) for TUM Stage-1 Aptitude Assessment.
